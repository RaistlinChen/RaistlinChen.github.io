Dark Chess (Banqi / Half Chess) is a Chinese board game played on a 4×8 grid. All 32 pieces start face-down and are revealed during play.

Strategy, incomplete information, and sharp tactical swings make every game different. The AI engine uses Expectiminimax search to handle reveal randomness — you'll face a real opponent that calculates and pressures, not random moves.
